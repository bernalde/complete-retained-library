# Third-party notices

Dependencies are not vendored in this snapshot. Lake retrieves the exact Git
revisions declared in `lean-modern/lake-manifest.json`; each dependency retains
its own licence and notices.

The formalization imports mathlib and Batteries. Copies of their Apache-2.0
licence texts are retained unchanged in `lean-modern/LICENSES/`. These notices
do not change the terms of other dependencies. Consult the upstream source at
the pinned revision for the complete component notices. Lean itself is obtained
separately through the pinned toolchain.

The scholarly works discussed in the source comments retain their original
authors and publication rights. Bibliographic references identify context;
they do not imply coauthor or publisher endorsement of the formalization.
No thesis/publisher PDF, source template, figure collection, original article
text archive or third-party dependency source tree is supplied here.
